## Guide
  - To add a project, follow the pattern established by the existing projects (pay particular attention to the information in the header). 
  - To volunteer to work on a project (or ask questions), contact the potential advisors listed on the project.
  - Another source for projects is [github issues](https://github.com/Macaulay2/M2/issues) (pay attention to the labels).
